ERRATIC ENFANTS — ERIN'S APP VERSION
Updated: 24 October 2026

Open Erratic-Enfants-App-Preview.html in Chrome or Edge to review the app.
Choose Begin and a feeling. Recharge appears after Reflect (10 seconds)
and Release (90 seconds), then runs for 20 active seconds.

Recharge now includes two optional movie links:
School of Rock — Learning in Song
https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/school_of_rock/videos/ScT_OYLuqaPK
An example of finding another way into learning when a lesson needs to change.

Freedom Writers — I Am Home
https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/freedom_writers/videos/FCnSJ1fiwyJF
An example of the sense of belonging a teacher can help create.

The links need internet access and open the full source clips in a new tab.
They are not edited 20-second video files. Clicking a link pauses an active
reset; return to the app tab and tap Resume. After completion, both links
remain available. No footage is downloaded, embedded, or rehosted.

Editable app code is in dist/:
  index.html — structure and the two Recharge links
  styles.css — fixed 4:5 canvas and layout
  app.mjs — buttons, steps, and pausing for external clips
  session.mjs — 10/90/20-second session timing
  content.mjs — check-in-specific teacher support
  assets/ — original icon and wave sound
